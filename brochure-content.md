## 4. Conflicts to resolve before printing

The old brochure and the website describe the same business differently. These
need a decision, not a copy-paste.

| | Old brochure (seo.pdf) | Website |
|---|---|---|
| **Contract terms** | "Minimum 3-month engagement", "no lock-in **after month three**", "cancel with 30 days' notice" | "**No lock-in contract**", "increase, pause or stop **at any time**", pause with **a week's** notice |
| Entity | BlueOrbit Digital Pvt. Ltd. | Blue Orbit Digi |
| Office | Kolkata, India | Implied Australia (Mon–Fri 9am–6pm AEST) |
| Phone | +91 94771 50805 | +61 2 6140 3441 |
| Domain | blueorbitdigi.com | blueorbitdigi.co |
| Markets | USA · Canada · India | UK · Ireland · Australia · USA · Canada |
| Years | 5+ | 7 |
| Scale | 140+ specialists | 50+ clients |

**The contract terms conflict matters most.** The website's single strongest
selling point is "no lock-in, stop any time" — it is in the hero headline, the
stat row, and the footer of every pricing section. The brochure contradicts it
with a three-month minimum. A prospect who reads both will notice, and it
undermines the one claim the whole site is built on. Pick one and make it true
everywhere.

**Pricing that is only in the brochure** (not on the website — add it to the
site or drop it from the brochure):
- Canada rate card: Starter $120 · Growth $250 · Premium $400 /mo
- Bundle pricing: Basic $800 · Growth $1,500 · Premium $2,500 /mo
- "No setup fee", "first audit free"

**Good news:** the Google Business Listing tier prices ($150 / $300 / $500) and
every deliverable in that table match the website exactly. That service is
already consistent across both.

**Statistics to verify:** the 90M / 89.5% / $164M figures on page 2 carry no
source. Check them before reprinting — an SEO firm quoting unsourced numbers is
an easy thing for a prospect to check and hold against you.
